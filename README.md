### 📦 Selected Projects and Software
#### [`GDM-Postnatal-Registry`](https://github.com/ruhaifimd/GDM-Postnatal-Registry)
**Postnatal e-registry for gestational diabetes follow-up.**
A web app that helps health staff track women with a history of Gestational Diabetes Mellitus after delivery, from the 6-week postnatal OGTT to annual screening, in line with the Malaysian CPG on Management of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (6th ed., 2020).  
#### [`Kursus-Generatif-AI`](https://github.com/ruhaifimd/Kursus-Generatif-AI)
**Generative AI course for health staff.**
Interactive, Malay-language training modules on Gemini and NotebookLM with live audience questions, introducing public health teams to safe and practical use of generative AI.  
#### [`Program-Registry-System`](https://github.com/ruhaifimd/Program-Registry-System)
**Course registration and attendance.**
Lightweight web tool for registering participants and recording attendance at health training programmes.  
- **Other contributions:** [`Multiple-Linear-Regression_DrPH`](https://github.com/ruhaifimd/Multiple-Linear-Regression_DrPH) and [`Multiple-Logistic-Regression_DrPH`](https://github.com/ruhaifimd/Multiple-Logistic-Regression_DrPH) (statistical modelling notebooks).
