## Selected Projects

### [GDM Postnatal Registry](https://github.com/ruhaifimd/GDM-Postnatal-Registry)

A web application designed to support follow-up for women with a history of gestational diabetes, from the six-week postnatal oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT) to annual screening. The intended workflow draws on the Malaysian CPG on Management of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (6th ed., 2020).

### [Generative AI for Health Staff](https://github.com/ruhaifimd/Kursus-Generatif-AI)

Bahasa Melayu course materials introducing Gemini and NotebookLM, with a focus on safe and practical generative AI use for the public health workforce.

### Public Health Statistical Modelling

R/Quarto group coursework in the DrPH programme: [multiple linear regression](https://github.com/ruhaifimd/Multiple-Linear-Regression_DrPH) and [multiple logistic regression](https://github.com/ruhaifimd/Multiple-Logistic-Regression_DrPH).

### [Program Registry System](https://github.com/ruhaifimd/Program-Registry-System)

A supporting web project for participant registration and attendance at health training programmes.

These repositories share software and learning materials; inclusion here does not establish clinical validation or suitability for handling identifiable health data.
